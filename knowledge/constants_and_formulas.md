# Physical constants (SI 2019 values)

Speed of light in vacuum: c = 299,792,458 m/s (exact).

Planck constant: h = 6.62607015e-34 J·s (exact). Reduced Planck constant: ħ = 1.054571817e-34 J·s.

Elementary charge: e = 1.602176634e-19 C (exact).

Boltzmann constant: k = 1.380649e-23 J/K (exact).

Avogadro constant: N_A = 6.02214076e23 per mole (exact).

Gravitational constant: G = 6.67430e-11 m^3 kg^-1 s^-2 (measured, uncertainty about 2e-5 relative).

Standard gravity: g = 9.80665 m/s^2 (conventional value for Earth surface gravity).

Electron mass: 9.1093837015e-31 kg. Proton mass: 1.67262192369e-27 kg.

Molar gas constant: R = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K). Stefan–Boltzmann constant: 5.670374419e-8 W m^-2 K^-4.

Astronomy: 1 astronomical unit = 149,597,870,700 m (exact). Earth mass about 5.972e24 kg, mean radius about 6,371 km. Sun mass about 1.989e30 kg. One light-year is about 9.4607e15 m. Absolute zero is -273.15 °C (0 K).

# Common formulas

Newton's second law: F = m a. Kinetic energy: KE = 1/2 m v^2. Momentum: p = m v. Weight: W = m g.

Ohm's law: V = I R. Electrical power: P = V I = I^2 R = V^2 / R. Resistors in series add: R = R1 + R2. Resistors in parallel: 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2.

Capacitor energy: E = 1/2 C V^2. Capacitor charge: Q = C V. RC time constant: tau = R C.

Mass–energy equivalence: E = m c^2. Photon energy: E = h f = h c / wavelength. Wave speed: v = f wavelength.

Gravity: F = G m1 m2 / r^2. Escape velocity: v = sqrt(2 G M / r). Schwarzschild radius of a black hole: r_s = 2 G M / c^2; for one solar mass it is about 2.95 km.

Ideal gas law: P V = n R T. Kinetic theory: average kinetic energy per particle = 3/2 k T.
